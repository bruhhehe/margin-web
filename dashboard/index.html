      Delete account

      Deleting your account removes everything Margin keeps for it: your shops and their eBay connections, the listings Margin watches, check history, costs you typed in, orders you marked, your layouts and your theme.

      • Your listings stay live on eBay. Margin stops managing them, so their prices and stock won't be updated anymore.
      • The Margin panel in Chrome will be signed out.
      • This can't be undone.

      Error reports

      Off unless you turn it on. When it's on and something on this page breaks, it sends the error, where it happened, the site version, and your browser version.

      Never your listings, orders, buyers, email, or passwords. Emails, numbers, and addresses are removed first. Connection problems aren't sent.

      Only Margin's developer sees them. Each is deleted 30 days after it last happened. At most 10 an hour. The panel has its own switch in Settings.

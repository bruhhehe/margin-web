List in seconds from any supported store.

Open the extension on any product page. Margin imports the title, images, price, variations and specifications, and drafts the listing for you. Review it, fix anything flagged, and publish.

  • Bulk listing. List a whole search, best-seller list or spreadsheet in one batch. Products you already sell on eBay are skipped.
  • Product finder. Find ranks Amazon’s best sellers, trending products and new releases by the profit a sale would leave you.
  • Flagged before you publish. Brands that enforce their trademarks, banned items, and words like “replica” in the title.

Bulk is on Pro and Max. Find is on Max.

Price by margin, with the fees built in.

Choose a percentage or a fixed amount. Every price includes eBay’s fees for your site and category, so you see your profit before you publish.

  • Change any number. Type a new price, fee, profit or margin, and the rest update to match.
  • Never below your minimum profit when the store changes its price.
  • No surprise listing fees. Margin asks eBay what a listing will cost before it goes live, and stops you if there’s a fee.

Stock and price tracking.

Margin checks the store for price, stock, and delivery time changes and updates your listings to match. When the store sells out, the listing is set to out of stock instead of ended, and goes back on sale when the store restocks.

  • Your listings stay live. Margin never ends a listing, so you keep its watchers and sales history.
  • Checked when Chrome is closed. Margin’s server checks your AliExpress products every hour. Turn on Help other sellers, and other Margin sellers check your Amazon products too.
  • Out of stock is on every plan. Back on sale by itself is on Pro and Max.

Delivery times that match the store.

When the store’s delivery time changes, Margin moves the listing to the shipping policy that matches, so your buyers see the right date. If a product gets too slow, Margin can take it off sale until it’s faster again.

  • Works on eBay UK, US and Australia.
  • Set it up once. Choose which shipping policy each delivery time uses.

Orders and shipping.

Every sale shows what to buy, where it goes, and when it’s due. You place the order and get it to your buyer. Margin handles the listing, the price and the checks.

  • See what is due. Closest deadline first, overdue in red. The count shows on the toolbar icon, so you see new sales without opening the panel.
  • Buy it without retyping. Opens the product page with the quantity set and fills in the buyer’s address at checkout. You place the order yourself.
  • Send tracking to eBay. Margin reads the tracking number from your order and detects the carrier. Send one order, or all of them at once.
  • Automatic ordering on AliExpress on Max, up to a limit you set for each order.

Track profit across sales, fees and costs in one place.

See the profit on every Amazon and AliExpress search result before you click. The dashboard adds up your sales, eBay fees, item costs and profit for any period.

  • A page for every listing, with its price history and every change Margin made.
  • Up to five eBay shops on Max, on one login.
  • The leaderboard. See where your store ranks among Margin sellers.

Questions people ask first.

Is this allowed under eBay’s dropshipping rules?

eBay has its own rules on dropshipping, including where you can buy the item and who ships it to your buyer, and they can differ by site. Margin doesn’t check your orders against them, and following them is up to you. Read eBay’s dropshipping policy for your site before you list.

Do checks run when my computer is off?

Your AliExpress products, yes. Margin’s server checks them every hour once Chrome has been closed for an hour.

Your Amazon products are checked in Chrome, and Chrome keeps checking after you close its windows, as long as the computer is on. Turn on Help other sellers in Settings, and other Margin sellers check your Amazon products while your computer is off.

Is my seller data safe?

Your eBay sign-in key is stored encrypted on Margin’s server, never in the extension. Amazon pages are read in your own browser, and nothing leaves your browser except to Margin and to eBay.

No analytics and no trackers, on this page or in the panel. Error reports stay off unless you turn them on. Your data is never sold.

Is there anything to set up?

No. Install it, create an account, connect your eBay shop, and list. There’s nothing else to install or configure.

Your eBay sign-in key is stored encrypted on Margin’s server, not in the extension, because a browser extension is the wrong place to keep the key to your shop.

How accurate is the fee?

It is an estimate, and it has to be. eBay calculates the real fee only after the sale happens, and tells you afterward. You have to price the listing before that, so this is the best figure to price against.

Rates come from each site’s published table, per category, with the regulatory operating fee included. eBay UK’s rates are checked against real order statements. If your invoice says otherwise, you can price against the percentage and fixed fee it shows instead. After a sale, the Orders tab shows what eBay actually charged.

Which eBay sites does it work on?

Fourteen: the United Kingdom, United States, Ireland, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Poland, Canada and Australia. Each has its own currency and fee table.

Business and private sellers pay different fees, so Margin asks eBay and remembers the answer for a day. A private seller on eBay UK pays no final value fee. On eBay US, they pay what a business pays. If eBay doesn’t say, Margin assumes business. Quoting no fee when you will be charged one is the expensive way to be wrong.

Does it use Amazon’s API?

No — neither the Product Advertising API nor the Creators API. Margin reads pages in your own browser, signed in as you. That is also the only approach that works: Amazon shows a captcha instead of a product page to requests from a data center.

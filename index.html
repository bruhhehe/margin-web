Key features

What Margin does, and what makes it different.

  • Easy to use

    Open the panel on a product page and the listing is already drafted: title, photos, item specifics, variations and price.

  • Stock and price tracking

    Exclusive

    Margin checks the store for price, stock, and delivery time changes and updates your listings to match. When the store sells out, the listing is set to out of stock instead of ended, and goes back on sale when the store restocks.

  • Private by design

    Amazon pages are read in your own browser. No analytics, no trackers, and your data is never sold. Your eBay connection is stored encrypted.

  • Fees built in

    Every price includes eBay’s fees for your site and category, so you see your profit before you publish.

  • Bulk listing

    List a whole search, best-seller list or spreadsheet in one batch. Products you already sell on eBay are skipped.

  • Product finder

    Find ranks Amazon’s best sellers, trending products and new releases by the profit a sale would leave you.

  • Orders and tracking

    Buy each sale on Amazon with the buyer’s address filled in at checkout. Margin reads the tracking number and sends it to eBay.

  • Works with Amazon and AliExpress

    Import from 22 Amazon sites and AliExpress, and list on 14 eBay sites, each in its own currency with its own fees.

Orders and shipping

Every sale shows what to buy, where it goes, and when it’s due. You place the order and get it to your buyer. Margin handles the listing, the price and the checks.

  1. See what is due

    Closest deadline first, overdue in red. The count shows on the toolbar icon, so you see new sales without opening the panel.

  2. Buy it without retyping

    Opens the product page with the quantity set and fills in the buyer’s address at checkout. You place the order yourself.

  3. Send tracking to eBay

    Margin reads the tracking number from your order and detects the carrier. Send one order, or all of them at once.

Your seller data stays yours.

Margin holds a key to your eBay shop. Here is what it does with it.

  • Your eBay sign-in key is stored encrypted on Margin’s server, never in the extension
  • No analytics and no trackers, on this page or in the panel. Error reports stay off unless you turn them on
  • Nothing talks to Amazon except your own browser. Product pages are read there, signed in as you
  • Nothing leaves your browser except to Margin and to eBay
  • Your data is never sold. There is no other business model here

Questions people ask first.

Is this allowed under eBay’s dropshipping rules?

eBay has its own rules on dropshipping, including where you can buy the item and who ships it to your buyer, and they can differ by site. Margin doesn’t check your orders against them, and following them is up to you. Read eBay’s dropshipping policy for your site before you list.

Is there anything to set up?

No. Install it, create an account, connect your eBay shop, and list. There’s nothing else to install or configure.

Your eBay sign-in key is stored encrypted on Margin’s server, not in the extension, because a browser extension is the wrong place to keep the key to your shop.

How accurate is the fee?

It is an estimate, and it has to be. eBay calculates the real fee only after the sale happens, and tells you afterward. You have to price the listing before that, so this is the best figure to price against.

Rates come from each site’s published table, per category, with the regulatory operating fee included. eBay UK’s rates are checked against real order statements. If your invoice says otherwise, you can price against the percentage and fixed fee it shows instead. After a sale, the Orders tab shows what eBay actually charged.

Which eBay sites does it work on?

Fourteen: the United Kingdom, United States, Ireland, Germany, France, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands, Belgium, Austria, Switzerland, Poland, Canada and Australia. Each has its own currency and fee table.

Business and private sellers pay different fees, so Margin asks eBay and remembers the answer for a day. A private seller on eBay UK pays no final value fee. On eBay US, they pay what a business pays. If eBay doesn’t say, Margin assumes business. Quoting no fee when you will be charged one is the expensive way to be wrong.

Does it use Amazon’s API?

No — neither the Product Advertising API nor the Creators API. Margin reads pages in your own browser, signed in as you. That is also the only approach that works: Amazon shows a captcha instead of a product page to requests from a data center.

9. Ordering on AliExpress

Ordering on AliExpress through Margin is part of the Max plan. If you connect AliExpress and press Order on AliExpress, or turn on Order on AliExpress automatically:

  • Margin places orders on your own AliExpress account, at your instruction and according to your settings. You are the buyer in each purchase. Margin is not a party to it and never holds your money.
  • With Pay automatically on, AliExpress charges the payment method saved in your AliExpress account, up to the maximum you set. With it off, you pay for each order on AliExpress yourself.
  • Ordering is best effort. An order may be delayed, fail, cost more than expected, or not be placed at all, because of AliExpress, eBay, stock, prices, addresses or a fault in Margin. Margin doesn’t retry a failed order by itself.
  • Review every order: that it was placed, what it cost, where it is going and its tracking. Fix anything wrong with AliExpress or your buyer yourself.
  • Disputes, refunds, returns and delivery problems are between you and AliExpress, and between you and your buyer.
  • Making sure that ordering this way is allowed by eBay’s dropshipping policy and AliExpress’s terms is up to you.

The same applies when the Orders tab helps you buy at another store: the extension fills in the details, and you place and pay for the order yourself.
